# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, or technology preference.

## Expected behavior

Be specific, constructive, and kind. Critique the work, not the person. Respect project scope, maintainer time, disclosure embargos, and support boundaries. Protect private information and obtain consent before sharing another person's details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sustained personal attacks, and publishing private or embargoed information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when behavior threatens the community or project. Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through a private contact method shown on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing personal details.
